**Privacy Policy for ChatGPT Prompt Navigator Extension** **Effective Date:** April 29, 2025 Thank you for using the ChatGPT Prompt Navigator Chrome Extension. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights. --- ### 1. **Data Collection and Usage** **1.1 Storage Permission:** We use Chrome's `storage` permission solely to save user preferences such as sidebar size, theme colors, anchor position, and other UI settings. No personal data or browsing history is stored or transmitted. **1.2 clipboardWrite Permission:** We use `clipboardWrite` to allow users to copy prompts and responses to their clipboard. This is a one-way action and we do not read or store clipboard content. **1.3 Host Permissions (`https://chatgpt.com/*`):** These permissions allow the extension to interact with the ChatGPT interface for the purpose of identifying and organizing your prompts and responses. No data is sent to any server or third-party service. **1.4 External Scripts:** If the user chooses to enable the "Buy Me a Coffee" donation button, we load a static external script from BuyMeACoffee.com for rendering that button. No personal data is shared or collected through this script. --- ### 2. **Data Sharing** We do not collect, store, or share any user data with third parties. All operations occur locally on the user’s browser. --- ### 3. **User Rights** - You have full control over your data. - You can clear extension settings anytime via Chrome > Extensions > Details > Clear Data. --- ### 4. **Contact Us** If you have questions or concerns about this policy, contact us at: 📧 promptnavigator@gmail.com --- ### 5. **Changes to This Policy** We may update this policy for clarity or compliance. All updates will be posted here with the effective date. --- Thank you for trusting ChatGPT Prompt Navigator. — The Developer
